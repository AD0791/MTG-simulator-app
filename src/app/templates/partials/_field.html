{# One markup contract for all seven inputs. Duplicating field markup is how error display drifts between fields. The control is `type="text"` with a numeric `inputmode` rather than `type="number"`: a number input silently discards a value it cannot parse, and a rejected submission has to come back with exactly what the reader typed. Validation is the server's job either way. {% endif %}
