If most of the capital sits outside the trading account, the account only has to cover the losses you mean to survive. Each row is the total staked through that many straight losses, up to the run's entry cap. Keeping the rest elsewhere does not move the wall: one loss more than the account covers still empties it. It only keeps the streak away from what you held back.
